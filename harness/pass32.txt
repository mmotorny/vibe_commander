The scenarios that build/portable_c32/VC.EXE passes, of those that it is
taken through: each as the original's golden has it, with what is
declared for the tree made to that (harness/deviations.py).
harness/ported.py wrote this, from a run of them all. The test
portable_c32-scenarios holds the program to it: it fails for a scenario
that is here and fails, and for one that passes and is not here.
`python3 harness/ported.py --record build/portable_c32/VC.EXE` adds what
has come to pass, and takes nothing away: a scenario leaves the record
by hand, with the reason in the commit.
